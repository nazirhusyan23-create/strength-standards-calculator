This policy explains what information Strength Standards ("this site," "we," "us") collects when you visit, how it's used, and the choices you have. By using this site you agree to the practices described below.

Information you enter into the calculators

The calculators ask for bodyweight, sex, and lift details (weight lifted, reps, or hold time) so they can estimate a one-rep max and a strength tier. Those values are sent to our server to be calculated and the result is returned to your browser — they are not stored, logged against you, or linked to any identity. We don't require an account, and there's no database of calculator submissions.

Log data

Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically records standard technical information for every request — IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, and timestamps — used only for security, abuse prevention, and understanding aggregate traffic. This is not tied to the values you enter into a calculator.

Cookies and similar technologies

This site itself doesn't set cookies to track you across visits. The advertising and analytics services described below may set their own cookies or similar identifiers in your browser, subject to their own policies and to your consent choices where required by law.

Advertising — Google AdSense

This site shows ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, device identifiers, or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites, and to measure ad performance.

  • You can review or opt out of personalized advertising through Google's own settings at adssettings.google.com.
  • You can also visit aboutads.info/choices to opt out of participating third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising.
  • More detail on how Google uses data from sites that use its services is available at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Third-party ad vendors, including Google, may also use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this website or other websites. This site does not control the cookies set by these third parties; their use is governed by their own privacy policies.

Analytics

We may use standard web analytics tools to understand aggregate traffic patterns — which pages are visited, roughly how long, and from what general region or device type. This data is aggregated and is not used to identify you personally.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this site, please contact us using the details below and we'll remove it.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of personal data collected about you, and to object to or restrict certain processing, including personalized advertising. Because this site does not maintain accounts or store your calculator inputs, there is generally no personal data tied to you to retrieve or delete beyond standard log data and any advertising identifiers managed by Google and its partners — see the links above to manage those directly. You can also reach out using the contact details below with any privacy request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example to reflect changes in the advertising services we use or in applicable law. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the site after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
